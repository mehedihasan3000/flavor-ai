# 1. Smart Meal Planning

**1.1 Weekly Meal Planner**

Let users plan:

Monday → Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner

through the entire week.

**1.2 AI Weekly Meal Plan**

User gives:

calorie target
protein target
dietary preference
available ingredients
number of people
budget

AI generates a complete weekly plan.

**1.3 Meal Plan Regeneration**

"Replace Wednesday dinner."

AI replaces only that meal while keeping the rest of the plan consistent.

**1.4 Meal Plan Optimization**

Allow users to say:

"Use my existing ingredients as much as possible."

AI reorganizes the week to minimize additional purchases.

**1.5 Leftover-Aware Meal Planning**

Example:

Monday: Roast chicken
Tuesday: Chicken sandwich
Wednesday: Chicken fried rice

This deliberately reuses leftovers instead of treating every meal independently.

**1.6 Calorie/Macro-Constrained Meal Planner**

Generate a day/week while targeting:

2,000 kcal + 140g protein

rather than calculating nutrition only after recipes are selected.

**1.7 Family Meal Planner**

Different family members can have different preferences.

Example:

User → high protein
Child → mild
Partner → vegetarian

AI creates compatible meals.

**1.8 Repeat/Favorite Meal Plans**

Save:

"My High-Protein Week"

and reuse it later.

# 2. Smart Grocery System*

This naturally follows meal planning. Modern meal-planning apps commonly generate organized shopping lists directly from meal plans.

**2.1 Automatic Grocery List**

Meal Plan →

Generate Grocery List

automatically.

**2.2 Pantry-Aware Grocery List**

If the user already has:

2 onions

and the plan needs:

5 onions

the grocery list shows:

Buy 3 onions

**2.3 Grocery Categories**

Automatically organize:

Vegetables
Meat
Dairy
Spices
Grains
Frozen
Snacks
**2.4 Grocery List Checking**

Users can check ingredients while shopping.

**2.5 Shared Grocery List**

Share with:

spouse
family
roommate
cooking partner
**2.6 Quantity Consolidation**

If three recipes need:

1 onion
2 onions
3 onions

show:

6 onions

instead of three separate items.

**2.7 Grocery Budget**

Set:

Weekly budget: $50

Then AI tries to keep the generated meal plan within that budget.

**2.8 Grocery Cost Estimation**

Estimate:

Total → $42

and show approximate cost per meal.

# 3. Real Pantry Management

Your current pantry matching is generation-focused. A persistent pantry would make it much more useful.

**3.1 Personal Pantry**

Users maintain:

Chicken: 500g
Rice: 2kg
Eggs: 8
Milk: 1L

**3.2 Pantry Quantity Tracking**

Users can increase/decrease quantities.

**3.3 Pantry Expiry Dates**

Example:

Milk expires in 2 days.

**3.4 Expiry Notifications**

"Your spinach expires tomorrow. Here are 3 recipes."

**3.5 Use-What-I-Have Mode**

User presses:

"Use my pantry"

and FlavorAI generates recipes using the ingredients most urgently needing consumption.

**3.6 Pantry Waste Report**

Example:

14 ingredients used
3 ingredients expired
Estimated waste reduced: X items

**3.7 Barcode/QR Scanning**

Scan packaged foods directly into the pantry.

**3.8 Pantry From Photo**

Take a photo of your kitchen/fridge and let AI identify ingredients.
